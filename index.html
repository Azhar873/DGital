What is CRM?

CRM, or Customer Relationship Management, refers to strategies, technologies, and tools that businesses use to manage and analyze customer interactions throughout the customer lifecycle. The goal of CRM is to improve business relationships, assist in customer retention, and drive sales growth. For e-commerce platforms like eBay and Amazon, CRM helps sellers streamline operations, personalize customer service, track sales performance, and maintain organized communication. By integrating CRM systems, businesses can offer better customer experiences, automate workflows, and gain valuable insights into consumer behavior.
